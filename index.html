Innovative Lattice Structure Design

The 765 kilovolt lattice dead-end tower is a vital component of the PowerOn Midwest transmission line, designed to efficiently transport power over long distances. Standing between 150 and 175 feet tall and spanning 140 to 150 feet wide, this resilient structure minimizes environmental impact by requiring fewer towers than lower-capacity lines. This technology provides critical backup power pathways to consistently deliver energy across the region.
